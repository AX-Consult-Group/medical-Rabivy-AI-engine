# Rep Talking Points by HCP Segment
Field reference. Scripted messaging aligned to the propensity model and GTM segments. Match the talking points to the HCP's profile in their account snapshot.

## High-Volume Obesity Prescribers
Who they are: the highest-frequency GLP-1 writers, often obesity medicine specialists or obesity-focused GPs. Priority targets for driving volume at launch.
Lead message: durability and plateau. "Your patients who have plateaued on their current weekly agent are the clearest fit - Rabivy showed continued weight loss through 52 weeks in Phase 2, without the plateau."
Support: monthly dosing reduces the touchpoints needed to keep a large panel adherent.
Signal to reference: high monthly Rx, high NRx share, high propensity decile.

## Competitive Switchers (Novo- or Lilly-heavy panels)
Who they are: HCPs whose panels are concentrated on Ozempic/Wegovy or Mounjaro/Zepbound but who may switch for tolerability, convenience, or efficacy.
Lead message: convenience. "For patients struggling with weekly injection burden or adherence, monthly dosing is a concrete reason to reassess."
Support: dual GLP-1/GIPR mechanism and no observed 52-week plateau.
Signal to reference: high Novo or Lilly panel share, active writer, moderate-to-high propensity.
Handle with care: do not disparage competitor products; frame as a fit for a specific patient type.

## HCPs with an Existing AX Pharmaceuticals Relationship
Who they are: clinicians already prescribing an AX Pharmaceuticals product (e.g. Repatha) - trust is established. Fastest path to first NRx.
Lead message: continuity. "You already know AX Pharmaceuticals' support model from Repatha - we'll bring the same access and patient-support infrastructure to Rabivy."
Support: leverage the existing rep relationship and known reliability.
Signal to reference: AX Pharmaceuticals relationship One or Two+, warm engagement score.

## Lapsed / Re-Engagement Patients
Who they are: HCPs managing patients who discontinued a GLP-1 due to side effects, cost, or adherence - already category-aware and high-intent for a better-tolerated option.
Lead message: a second chance at therapy. "For patients who came off a weekly agent, Rabivy's monthly dosing and optimised dose escalation directly address the two most common reasons they quit."
Support: adherence and tolerability framing.
Signal to reference: category-aware panel, discontinuation history in the territory.

## Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs)
Who they are: influential clinicians and researchers whose endorsement shapes peer prescribing. Reached through congresses, advisory boards, and publications.
Lead message: shape the narrative early. Focus on mechanism novelty (GLP-1 agonism + GIPR antagonism) and durability data.
Support: invite to advisory boards and data-review sessions; this is a seeding relationship, not a volume play.

## Access-Constrained HCPs
Who they are: high-propensity clinicians whose patient panel faces formulary or PA barriers (often Medicare/Medicaid-heavy).
Lead message: support, not just science. "We know access is the hurdle here - here's how our hub and PA-support resources shorten time to first fill."
Signal to reference: formulary status Not Covered or PA Required, high PA burden.
