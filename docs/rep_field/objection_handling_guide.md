# Objection Handling Guide
Field reference. Common HCP objections and recommended responses. Keep responses factual and within promotional guidance; Rabivy is investigational.

## "I'm already happy with Ozempic / Zepbound."
Acknowledge, then differentiate on patient fit, not product quality. "Those are strong agents. The question is which of your patients struggle with weekly injection burden or have plateaued - that subgroup is where monthly dosing and the dual mechanism are most relevant." Redirect to a specific patient type rather than a blanket switch.

## "Monthly dosing doesn't matter that much."
Reframe around persistence data. Real-world GLP-1 persistence is poor: a large share of patients discontinue within the first year, with adherence and injection burden among the drivers. Fewer injections is a lever on the biggest problem in the category - keeping patients on therapy long enough to benefit.

## "Cost and prior authorization are the real barriers."
Agree, and shift to support. "You're right that access is the bottleneck. That's why the focus isn't just the molecule - it's the hub, PA support, and coverage strategy behind it." For Medicare/Medicaid-heavy panels, be honest that obesity coverage is still evolving and set expectations accordingly. Do not overstate coverage.

## "GLP-1s cause too many GI side effects; my patients can't tolerate them."
Address with the dose-escalation and mechanism angle. "Rabivy uses an optimised dose-escalation approach designed to manage tolerability, and the mechanism differs from pure GLP-1 agonism." Do not claim a superior safety profile beyond available Phase 2 data.

## "The weight comes back when patients stop."
This is the plateau and rebound message. Phase 2 showed continued weight loss through 52 weeks without the plateau seen with existing agents. Frame durability as the differentiator, while noting Phase 3 data will further characterise this.

## "It's not approved yet - why are we talking?"
Be transparent. "You're right, it's investigational and in Phase 3. My goal today is to make sure you know the mechanism and the Phase 2 data now, so you can evaluate it properly when it launches." Never imply approval.

## "How is this different from tirzepatide? That's also a dual mechanism."
Clarify the mechanism precisely. Tirzepatide is a GLP-1 and GIP receptor *agonist*. Rabivy combines GLP-1 receptor agonism with GIP receptor *antagonism* - a different pharmacological approach - delivered on a monthly dosing schedule via an antibody conjugate. Keep this factual and avoid superiority claims.

## Compliance Reminder
Stay within available data. No off-label promotion, no unapproved claims, no head-to-head superiority statements beyond head-to-head evidence. When unsure, defer to medical affairs.
