# Rabivy Product & Benefits Brief
Field reference for AX Pharmaceuticals commercial teams. For internal use.

## What Rabivy Is
Rabivy (maridebart cafraglutide) is AX Pharmaceuticals' investigational antibody-peptide conjugate for the treatment of obesity and Type 2 diabetes. It pairs a monoclonal antibody against the GIP receptor (GIPR) with two peptide GLP-1 receptor agonist molecules, joined via amino acid linkers. Rabivy is in Phase 3 development and is not yet approved.

## Mechanism: Why It Is Different
Conventional GLP-1 therapies act through GLP-1 receptor agonism alone. Rabivy combines GLP-1 receptor agonism with GIP receptor antagonism. This dual mechanism is designed to drive stronger, more sustained weight loss and to blunt the weight-loss plateau seen with existing agents.

## The Headline Differentiator: Monthly Dosing
The antibody backbone gives Rabivy a long half-life (approximately 21 days), enabling monthly or less-frequent dosing. Every approved competitor - Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound - is a weekly injection. This is the single most important commercial message: same clinical category, materially lower injection burden.

## Key Benefits vs Weekly Injectables
- Monthly (or less frequent) dosing vs weekly injections
- Improved adherence potential from reduced injection frequency
- No weight-loss plateau observed at 52 weeks in Phase 2
- Reduced weight rebound
- Optimised dose escalation to manage tolerability

## Phase 2 Evidence (52 Weeks)
In a Phase 2 study, AX Pharmaceuticals reported robust weight loss with Rabivy in people living with obesity or overweight at 52 weeks, with weight loss continuing rather than plateauing at the end of the observation window. Detailed Phase 3 efficacy and safety data are pending. Reps should describe Phase 2 results as encouraging but investigational, and must not imply approval or make head-to-head superiority claims beyond available data.

## Positioning Summary
Rabivy competes in the established GLP-1 obesity and Type 2 diabetes category. Its wedge is convenience and durability of effect: monthly dosing addresses the adherence and persistence problem that erodes weekly-injectable patient populations over time, and the dual mechanism targets the plateau problem. Lead with dosing convenience; support with durability of weight loss.

## Compliance Note
Rabivy is investigational. All discussion must remain consistent with the approved label once available and with current promotional guidance. Do not promote off-label use. Do not present Rabivy as approved or as proven superior to marketed products outside of head-to-head data.
