# Rabivy Clinical Summary

Clinical reference. Investigational agent - Phase 2 data only; Phase 3 ongoing. For internal use; not promotional.

## Molecule and Mechanism

Rabivy (trelaglutide) is an antibody-peptide conjugate: a monoclonal antibody targeting the GIP receptor (GIPR), conjugated to two GLP-1 receptor agonist peptides via amino acid linkers. The design combines GLP-1 receptor agonism with GIP receptor antagonism. This differs from marketed dual-incretin therapy (tirzepatide), which is a GLP-1/GIP dual *agonist*. The antibody backbone confers a long half-life (\~21 days), enabling monthly or less-frequent subcutaneous dosing.

## Phase 2 Results (52 Weeks)

AX Pharmaceuticals reported robust weight loss with Rabivy in people living with obesity or overweight at 52 weeks. The notable feature was the trajectory: weight loss continued through the 52-week window rather than plateauing, in contrast to the plateau typically observed with existing GLP-1 agents over a comparable period. An optimised dose-escalation approach was used to manage gastrointestinal tolerability, consistent with the class.

## Tolerability

As with the GLP-1 class, gastrointestinal effects (nausea, vomiting, diarrhea) are the principal tolerability consideration. Dose escalation is used to mitigate these. Full safety characterisation, including discontinuation rates attributable to adverse events, awaits Phase 3 readouts.

## Efficacy Context vs Marketed Agents

For reference, marketed agents in pivotal trials: - Tirzepatide (SURMOUNT-1, 72 weeks): up to \~22.5% mean weight loss. - Semaglutide 2.4mg (STEP 1, 68 weeks): \~14.9% mean weight loss. These come from separate trials with different designs and populations and are not directly comparable to Rabivy's Phase 2 results. No head-to-head data exist. Avoid cross-trial superiority claims.

## Development Status

Rabivy is in Phase 3. Efficacy magnitude, durability beyond 52 weeks, and the full safety profile will be defined by Phase 3 readouts. All external communication must treat Rabivy as investigational.

## Key Clinical Messages (internal framing)

- Novel mechanism: GLP-1 agonism + GIPR antagonism.
- Monthly dosing enabled by antibody half-life.
- Continued weight loss without 52-week plateau in Phase 2.
- Dose escalation to manage tolerability.
