# Specialty Benchmark Profiles
Reference document. All figures derived from the Rabivy prescriber master dataset (n = 15,000 HCPs), reporting period June 2026.
Use these profiles to contextualise any individual HCP: 'compared with a typical endocrinologist' means compared with the averages below.

## Typical Primary Care Profile
- Population: 12,267 HCPs (82% of universe); 4,704 active GLP-1 writers (38%)
- Monthly GLP-1 Rx (active writers): mean 13, median 10
- New patient starts (active writers): mean 3.2 NRx/month (25% of volume)
- Rabivy propensity: mean 12/100 (active writers 16/100)
- Tier mix: High 6% | Medium 34% | Watch 60%
- Payer mix (avg): Commercial 30% | Medicare 40% | Medicaid 20% | OOP 10%
- Prior authorization burden (avg): 53%
- Estimated obesity prevalence in panel (modeled, avg): 22%
- AX Pharmaceuticals relationship: None 40% | One product 38% | Two+ products 22%
- On rep call plan: 29% | Years in practice (avg): 18

## How to Use These Benchmarks
An individual HCP writing materially above the specialty mean for monthly Rx or NRx is a high-value account for their specialty. A propensity score above the specialty average indicates above-typical likelihood to adopt Rabivy. Access comparisons should use the payer-mix and PA-burden benchmarks for the same specialty, since patient panels differ structurally across Primary Care, Endocrinology, and Obesity Medicine.
